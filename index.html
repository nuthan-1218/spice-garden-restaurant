OUR CULINARY HERITAGE

A Legacy Steeped in Precious Spices

✸

Spice Garden began with a singular reverence: that authentic Indian cooking is not merely about chili heat, but a centuries-old symphony of aromatics, slow heat, and deep cultural hospitality.

Our kitchen honors the legendary royal Dum Pukht techniques of the Nawabs of Awadh alongside the bold, sun-drenched coastal spices of the Chettiar merchants. Basmati grains are aged for a minimum of two years; whole black cardamom, Kashmiri saffron, and star anise are stone-pounded by hand to preserve the soul of their fragrance.

Here, every meal is served with the timeless Indian ethos of Atithi Devo Bhava—where every guest is revered as divine royalty.

Heritage Dum Recipes Preserving royal family secrets untouched by modern industrial shortcuts.
Zero Artificial Colors Only sun-dried Kashmiri chillies, pure turmeric, and golden saffron.
Warm Welcoming Spaces Intimate family booths, hand-carved jali partitions, and serene sitar notes.
